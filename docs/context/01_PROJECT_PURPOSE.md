# Purpose and intended user

The first user is a medicine procurement or supply-chain analyst investigating FDA-regulated products. VitaLink consolidates external evidence into an investigation that can be revisited, explained and exported. It is not a prescribing tool, supplier marketplace or freight booking service.

The capstone team has no supplier partnership, private hospital inventory feed or commercial carrier agreement. A useful application can still operate on real public records: identify an event, preserve its exact scope, connect documented products and companies, disclose uncertainty, and record an analyst's next action. Missing organisational data stays missing.

## The real use case

An analyst sees a shortage presentation or recall. National shortage status alone does not say whether that presentation is unavailable, and a recall can apply only to specified lots. VitaLink preserves those distinctions, opens a persistent case, checks the selected medicine's identity, shows the documented ingredient/labeler network and retains evidence and decisions together.

If the analyst supplies stock, interruption duration or offers, the app can compare conditional responses. Those results answer a stated scenario; they do not establish a hospital's actual stockout date. Without those inputs, the public evidence investigation remains useful on its own.

## Product boundaries

- Use real FDA records and real retrieved product data, with URLs and timestamps.
- Use the United States regulatory scope first. FDA status does not establish Indian market availability or local supply.
- Show REAL, DERIVED, MODELLED and SCENARIO quantities with their methods and assumptions.
- Keep reported product availability separate from commercial stock and delivery promises.
- Keep evidence of a company association separate from evidence of a physical supply dependency.
- Treat alternative products as candidates until authoritative equivalence and professional review support a substitution.
- Make proposed work visible as proposed; do not fill gaps with fictional suppliers, prices, facilities or routes.

The industrial inspiration is ZS Associates External Disruption Sensing (EDS): detect external change, relate it to exposure and support a response. This is inspiration, not an affiliation, equivalent commercial coverage or claim of independently novel technology. [Competitor research](10_COMPETITOR_RESEARCH.md) explains adjacent approaches.

The visual inspiration is [God's Eye View](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view): a dark spatial workspace with layered information panels. Visualisation must clarify [actual evidence](04_DATA_AND_EVIDENCE.md). Recalling-firm city points are not plant coordinates or live shipment tracks.
