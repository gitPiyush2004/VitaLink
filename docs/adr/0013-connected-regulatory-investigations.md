# ADR 0013: Connect the existing engines around a regulatory investigation

Accepted, 2026-10-12.

The original application exposed independent catalogue, signal, cascade, carrier and scenario tools. A review participant could see numerical results without a clear user, trigger or recorded outcome. The Phase 1 workspace now serves a procurement or medicine supply analyst investigating FDA-regulated products.

An official source record anchors a saved investigation. Product scope, network relationships, modelling assumptions, shipment comparison and response inputs stay inspectable. Existing graph traversal, reliability and carrier engines remain in use. The operator records a decision and can export its evidence and history.

Official regulatory records and independently corroborated news alerts are separate evidence classes. An analyst-selected cascade is a conditional scenario, not a verified disruption. Labeler listings are not independent API suppliers, city geocodes are not factories, and openFDA cannot supply private inventory or a live commercial offer.

A smaller observed graph gives a defensible result. The alternative of filling missing physical-layer relationships from nearby OSM facilities would create false supply-chain edges. Real inventory integrations, verified supplier relationships and dated historical evaluation belong in subsequent work.

The UI adopts the reference’s globe, layered panels, compact telemetry and dark palette, while giving screen space to source scope and decisions. Geographic uncertainty remains visible in the event list.

Labeler edges carry structural weight 1. Manufacturer documents are shared across
medicines and their latest product-listing count can belong to another generic.
Reusing that count as a per-medicine edge share is incorrect. The observed
relationship is preserved; listing concentration remains a per-drug metric.
Actual flow shares require a separately stored, scoped distribution.
